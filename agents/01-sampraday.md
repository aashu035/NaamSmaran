## Who We Are

- **Bhav:** Sahchari Bhav / Sakhi Bhav — we are सहचरी of Swamini Shri Radha Kishori

## The Philosophical Foundation

- **Tat-Sukh Bhav (तत्सुख भाव):** Every action aimed at the happiness of Shri
  PriyaLal — never for one's own pleasure. This is the essence of Radhavallabh Sampraday.
  This is the exact opposite of Swa-Sukh Bhav (स्वसुख भाव).
- **Rasopasana (रसोपासना):** The divine love between Shri PriyaPriyatam —
  Nikunj Lila, Van Vihar Lila, Raas Lila as described in Shri Hit Chaturasi Ji.
- **Radha Charan Pradhan:** Shri Radha's lotus feet are the primary focus — not
  a means to something else. Shri Krishna (Radhavallabh) exists for her delight.
- No fasting, no ritual in this tradition — pure Prema Bhakti only.

## Principal Scriptures Referenced in This App

1. **Shri Hit Chaturasi Ji** — 84 pads in Brajbhasha by Shri Hit Harivansh Mahaprabhu Ji.
   The principal text of Radhavallabh Sampraday. Describes divine pastimes of
   Shri PriyaPriyatam in Nivrit Nikunj.
2. **Shri Hit Radha Sudhanidhi Ji** — devotional nectar verses
3. **Shri Hit Sevak Vaani** — devotional verses for the seva companion
4. **Custom quotes** — user-addable

## Visual World — What Belongs in This App

ONLY imagery and references from within the Radhavallabh sampraday world:

- Vrindavan, Yamuna pulin (sacred riverbank), Nikunj (secret bower/grove)
- Van Vihar (forest walk), Sharad Purnima (autumn full moon)
- **Flowers:** champak, bakul, madhavi lata, malati, juhi, kadamba, lotus (pink and golden)
- **Birds:** peacock (mor), parrot (shuk/kir), cuckoo (pik), swan (hans)
- **Colors:** deep indigo, rose-gold, champak-gold, emerald green, moonlight silver,
  diya-flame amber, pearl white
- **Sacred names:** राधा, हरिवंश, राधावल्लभ, गुरु कृपा केवलं

## What is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN in This App

> [!CAUTION]
> Violating any of these rules corrupts the sacred purpose of this app.

- **No Om symbol (ॐ)** — ever, anywhere
- **No Trishul**, no Rudraksha, no Garuda, no conch (shankh) as decoration
- **No Shiva references**, no Durga references, no generic "Hindu" iconography
- **No depiction of deity form directly**
- **No Gaudiya Vaishnava symbols** or references (different sampraday)
- **No temple architecture** in backgrounds
- **Nothing outside** Shri Hit Harivansh Mahaprabhu's sampraday visual world
